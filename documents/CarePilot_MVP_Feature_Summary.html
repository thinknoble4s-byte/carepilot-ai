CarePilot AI: follow-up for breast cancer survivors

CarePilot keeps track of each patient's follow-up visits and tests, reminds patients on (simulated) WhatsApp in their own language, and hands anything to do with health to a person. It never diagnoses, reads results or gives advice.

AWS MumbaiSarvam AI (Indian languages) ~310 automated testsDemo profile: no login or security

1Patients and care plans

  • Patient import: the follow-up register and daily appointment list load from CSV; bad rows go to an exceptions list instead of being created silently.
  • Consent per patient: WhatsApp to the patient, to the caregiver, and automated messaging. No consent means nothing is sent.
  • Three care plans to choose from: T1 Hormone receptor positive, T2 Hormone receptor negative, T3 HER2 positive.
  • Plan schedule: visits at months 3, 6, 9 and 12, each with its tests (blood test, mammogram, DEXA, ultrasound, 2D Echo, PET-CT) due a week before.
  • Confirming a plan: the coordinator checks two identifiers; order cards are then created, each with a named owner and cover.

2Order cards (visits and tests)

Visits
Due→Booked→Attended
Blood test (specimen-based)
Due→Scheduled→Completed→Report received→Report verified
Imaging (non-specimen)
Due→Scheduled→Completed→Verified

Only the doctor sets a test to verified. Attendance comes only from the appointment list, never from a patient's message.

3Daily automation

  • 07:00 gap check: finds items not booked 7 days before they are due, and missed items.
  • 07:30 reminders: approved templates in Kannada, Hindi or English, copying the caregiver when they have consented.
  • Safety rules: no consent, a paused patient or the agent switched off means no message, and a reason is recorded.
  • Only one reminder per gap; repeat runs are suppressed.

4Patient replies (WhatsApp simulator)

  • Booked updates a visit card, or creates a task to add the date for a test.
  • Please help me book and I can't come each create a task for a person; typing HELP reaches a person.
  • Free text and voice notes: Sarvam AI transcribes, translates and asks one question: does this mention health?
    • Yes, unsure, or the AI fails: a fixed acknowledgement, handed to the coordinator, all automated messages stop.
    • No: the AI drafts a note for the coordinator to approve or reject.
  • The simulator looks like a WhatsApp chat (pending deploy).

5People and handoffs

  • Tasks each have an owner, a cover and a deadline in working days; a missed deadline goes to the cover.
  • Handover chain: the coordinator reviews, the doctor decides Urgent or Routine, then writes an instruction in the (simulated) hospital record, which creates a new visit card and a booking message.
  • Urgent call: "call the patient today", owned by the coordinator until 16:00, the cover until 18:00, then Overdue.
  • Call attempts are logged. Only a person closes a handover, with a reason.

6Tests and reports

  • Simulated hospital lab: reports linked by MRN and test code; a mismatch becomes a task, a report with no matching card goes to exceptions.
  • Outside reports: the patient sends a photo or PDF, held Unverified; CarePilot reads only identifiers, never results, and the coordinator checks two of them.
  • Doctor: opens the source file, verifies the test, and decides "Continue as planned" (the patient gets a message) or "Needs discussion".
  • An undecided item moves to the doctor's cover after one working day.

8Trust and demo tools

  • Audit trail: hash-chained record of every action, with who did it and the demo date and time; AI suggestions are labelled separately.
  • Demo clock: set the date and time, or run the 07:00 and 07:30 jobs on demand.
  • Reset demo: six data sets (Use Cases 1, 2a, 2b, 2c, 2T and the Golden Path), all starting with no plan chosen.

9Running it

  • Hosting: AWS Mumbai with a static website, one Lambda API and a PostgreSQL database.
  • Database: off by default, started only with approval, stops automatically when idle.
  • Testing: about 310 automated tests, plus a local demo that runs without AWS.
