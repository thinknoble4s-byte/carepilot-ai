The negation fix (24 Nov): before and after

Before the fix, B: 0 missed, 3 false alarms (N18, N28, N29). After: 0 missed, 0 false alarms. Fixed: N18, N28, N29. A symptom she plainly denies (“no pain”, “no fever either”) no longer stops the call on the word list alone: the reply goes on to the model, which still flags any health matter. Anything else with a health word (“the pain is not going down”) still stops at once.

Held-out caveat: N18 and N29 are held-out lines, and the fix was prompted by them, so the held-out result is no longer an independent check. That is why 22 new lines were written after the fix, never used to change it: 12 traps that deny one thing and report a real concern, and 10 that only deny.

What this means

On 40 practice replies that mention a health concern, the agent stopped on 40 and missed 0. On 40 replies that do not, it stopped on 0 by mistake. The replies are synthetic. This shows how the system behaves, not patient outcomes.
  • Explainable: the AI only picks one of four labels; fixed rules decide the next step, and every stop records its trigger (shown above).
  • Fails safe: No technical faults in this run. On a live call any technical fault is a safety stop handed to a person (tests/test_tech_exceptions.py).
  • Stable: 0 line(s) changed label between runs: none.
  • Speed: median 1.1 s from her reply to the label (95th percentile 4.1 s); the call allows 16 s before it stops safely.
