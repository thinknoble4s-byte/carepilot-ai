2Incoming messages: what happens, and the questions asked

When a patient sends a WhatsApp text or voice note, Sarvam turns speech into text and translates it. A model then tags it from fixed lists only.

2.1 Routing

Clearly not about health (no symptom, no severe word)One standard reply, no call:
Thank you for your message. This number is only for health concerns after your treatment, so we have not acted on it. For anything else, please contact the hospital helpdesk. If you feel unwell in any way, send us a voice message or reply HELP.
No Hindi version on file
About health, or unsureAcknowledgement, then an automated call within 2 minutes:
Thank you for your message. CarePilot's automated assistant will call you in the next 2 minutes with a few short questions. If you feel very unwell, go to the nearest emergency department. — Hospital A, Chennai (automated assistant)
No Hindi version on file
She uses a word like “severe”, “unbearable”The result can be no lower than Amber (floor AMBER_PATIENT_SAYS_SEVERE)
A symptom we have no questions forThe result can be no lower than Amber (floor AMBER_OTHER_SYMPTOM_REPORTED)
Tags missing or unreadableThe fixed 12 questions: Q_SLURRED, Q_AWAKE, Q_CHEST, Q_ARM_RED, Q_TEMP, Q_VOMIT, Q_HEADACHE, Q_HEAD_SCORE, Q_HEAD_WORSE, Q_BANGLES, Q_NAUSEA, Q_SUPP

2.2 Which questions a call asks

Always first, in this order: Q_SLURRED, Q_AWAKE, Q_CHEST, Q_ARM_RED, Q_TEMP. Then Q_BANGLES if her record has a left-arm baseline. Then a module for each symptom she mentioned:

She mentionsQuestions added
dizzy or visionQ_DIZZY, Q_VISION
fever or chillsQ_CHILLS, Q_FEELS_HOT
headacheQ_HEADACHE, Q_HEAD_SCORE, Q_HEAD_WORSE
nauseaQ_NAUSEA, Q_VOMIT, Q_LIQUIDS, Q_SUPP
sleepyQ_DROWSY
arm painQ_ARM_SCORE, Q_ARM_SUDDEN
arm swellingQ_SWOLLEN, Q_SKIN
tiredQ_TIRED_DAYS, Q_TIRED_WORSE
chest or breathingalready in the core
other symptomno questions: raises the floor to Amber

Skips: “headache today” = No skips the headache score and “worse than last time”. “Nausea since yesterday” = No skips the supplements question, and also the water question when she has not vomited. The call stops at the first confirmed warning sign.

danger questions: an answer the system cannot read (“don't know”, unclear) makes the case Red (unconfirmed). For other questions an unclear answer makes it Amber. Each spoken answer is read by fixed word lists first; the model is asked only when those fail, and its answer is checked.

4How the system classifies Red, Amber and Green

Plain rules in code, checked in this order. The first group that matches decides. Only questions that were actually asked can fire a rule.

Red

Confirmed warning sign (any one):
  • Speech reported as slurred, or confusion
  • Reported as so sleepy she cannot keep her eyes open
  • Chest pain or hard to breathe reported
  • Left arm reported as red or hot to touch
  • Temperature reported as 100.4 F (38.0 C) or more
  • Dizzy or unsteady when walking
  • Blurred vision reported
  • Shaking chills reported
  • Vomiting reported 6 or more times since yesterday
  • Reported as not able to keep water down
  • Headache reported as 7 out of 10 or more

Also Red: an unclear answer to a danger question (unconfirmed); she reported symptoms and 2 calls 5 minutes apart were not answered; an empty message, then 2 automated and 2 staff calls not answered; the system cannot read the answers.

Amber

No Red, and any one of:
  • Bangles or sleeve tighter on the left arm
  • Headache score 2+ above her last recorded score
  • Headache worse than last time
  • Nausea although supplements were taken after full meals
  • Sleepy or groggy most of today
  • Vomited 2 to 5 times since yesterday
  • Left arm swollen or heavy
  • Cut, bite or prick on the left arm
  • No thermometer and feels hot; or no thermometer with headache, nausea, sleepiness or vomiting
  • Left arm pain 7/10 or more
  • Arm pain started suddenly
  • Tired 7 days or more; or tiredness worse than last week
  • 3rd report in 14 days of headache, nausea or arm pain
  • An unclear answer to a non-danger question
  • She pressed 0 to ask for a doctor
  • Her own message said “severe”, or named a symptom we have no questions for
  • Empty message, 2 automated calls unanswered

Green

No rule fired.
  • Closing line; the doctor's own advice repeated if she reports nausea
  • Follow-up call the next morning
  • Coordinator reviews the Green list
