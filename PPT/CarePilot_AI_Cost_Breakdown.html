Points to check before submitting

4. What judges may ask

  1. One-time ₹4–6 lakh: based on 2 part-time developers (section 1a). Validate the day rate and the 35% time share.
  2. The running cost is for a production-grade setup (Multi-AZ database, NAT, WAF, backups). Today's MVP runs a smaller demo setup at about US$45–60 a month.
  3. Not included: telephony minutes, staff time, GST, and optional self-hosted Sarvam speech models (US$250–800 a month extra).
  4. Margin: the subscription brings in ₹30–50k against ₹18–23k of running cost at one hospital. It improves as hospitals share one platform: AWS is mostly a fixed cost, and only the Sarvam part grows with volume.
Cost controls in the MVP

5. Why the AI bill stays small

  • Button replies (Booked, Please help me book, I can't come) and the triage rules use no AI.
  • The fixed call questions are spoken once by Bulbul and cached for every later call.
  • The language model is asked only when the word lists cannot read an answer.
  • The automated call stops at the first danger sign, so fewer questions are asked.
  • Serverless Lambda is pay per use; the demo database stops when idle.
